# Business Process Models

## Purpose

This folder contains eight Visual Paradigm (`.vpp`) project files representing the operational and support workflows documented for the industrial organization. Together, the models describe activities, role handoffs, decision points, exceptions, and reporting paths across the business.

## Model catalogue

| Model file | Business process represented |
|---|---|
| `energy-management.vpp` | Energy monitoring, analysis of consumption, routine reporting, and escalation of sustained abnormal usage |
| `maintenance-management.vpp` | Equipment inspection, preventive maintenance planning, emergency repair authorization, and post-repair verification |
| `production-planning-and-control.vpp` | Review of production inputs and raw-material data, planning, collection of operational statistics, and periodic reporting |
| `product-production.vpp` | Ore/material inspection and production routing through crushing, drying, storage, and possible return to the production line |
| `quality-control.vpp` | Measurement against standards, remeasurement of nonconforming inputs, and preparation of quality reports |
| `human-resources.vpp` | Organizational chart and role definition, recruitment and candidate review, onboarding/training, and employee feedback |
| `financial-accounting.vpp` | Financial-document review and registration, payroll-related processing, treasury steps, and preparation of financial statements |
| `warehouse-management.vpp` | Inventory checks, shortage notification and purchasing requests, issuing materials, and updating stock records |

## How the models fit together

The processes are cross-functional rather than isolated: production depends on raw-material availability and quality; warehouse shortages can trigger purchasing; maintenance affects equipment readiness; and workforce, accounting, and energy processes provide supporting controls and management information. These models establish the business-process context for the database and reporting artifacts elsewhere in the repository.

Open the `.vpp` files with a compatible version of Visual Paradigm. The filenames use English descriptions; the original model files have been renamed, not recreated.
